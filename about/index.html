A translator, not a guru

Free AI lessons for anyone, one idea at a time. No hype. No jargon. Just help.

Why this exists

A few years back, a one-page infographic taught me how to Google: the minus sign, the tilde, date ranges. Little tricks most people never learned, and that was fine. A bad Google search only wastes a minute.

AI is different. Using it badly costs real money, health and reputation. Using it well pays off more than search ever did. So this is that infographic, rebuilt for AI, and for everyone.

Who it’s for

Anyone who has heard a lot about AI and been shown very little. People starting from zero, and people who want to build real things with it. The lessons start at the beginning and go as far as you want.

There are no stupid questions here, and nobody is behind.

What we promise

  • Free, forever. Every lesson is complete on its own, never a teaser for something paid.
  • One idea at a time. A few screens, one picture, nothing to wade through.
  • The catch, every time. Each lesson says what can go wrong or where the tool falls short.
  • A date on everything. Apps change, so every lesson is checked again every three months.
  • Your privacy first. We say what never to type in, early and often.

What to watch out for

We get things wrong too. Buttons move, free plans change, and a lesson can go stale between checks. If something here doesn’t match your screen, trust your screen.

Paid help for businesses may come one day. If it does, it will be separate and clearly marked. The lessons stay free and whole either way.

How lessons are checked

Every lesson goes through the same steps before it reaches you, and the last screen of each one says when its facts were checked.

Fact-checked
Every claim is checked against official pages and the original sources. Anything that can’t be confirmed is reworded or cut.
Signed off by a person
Nothing goes up until the person who runs this site has signed it off.
Tried by a beginner
A lesson is marked as still being tested until someone new to AI has worked through it.
Checked again
Apps change, so every lesson is checked again every three months, and dated.
