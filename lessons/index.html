Module 1: How we got here

Where AI came from, what it's made of, and why it behaves the way it does.

1A.1 The story so far

AI is older than you think

The family of words

AI, machine learning, neural networks and LLMs fit inside each other like nesting dolls. → 1C.4 Under the hood

Attention: reading every word in context

In 2017, a new design let a model weigh every word against every other word at once. → 1C.5 Under the hood

How it writes

It writes by picking a likely next word, over and over. Likely isn’t the same as true. → 1C.6 Under the hood

Tokens: how it reads

It doesn’t read letters or words. It reads chunks called tokens. → 1C.8 Under the hood

The context window: its working memory

It can only hold so much in mind at once. Long chats push the oldest parts out. → 1C.9 Under the hood

Why it makes things up

Open and closed models
