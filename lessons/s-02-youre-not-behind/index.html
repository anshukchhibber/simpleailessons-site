You’re not behind

Chat AI arrived at the end of 2022. Next to the web or the iPhone, we’re all beginners.

How it works

1

It’s still early.

The web has been public since 1991, and the iPhone since 2007. ChatGPT arrived at the end of 2022. On that scale, everyone is new at this.

How it works

2

It keeps changing.

New versions come out every few months. People who started in 2022 keep relearning too, so starting now costs less than you’d think.

How it works

3

You already have the hard part.

Knowing your own work, and noticing when something’s off, matters more than knowing the buttons. The buttons take a few weeks.

What experience teaches

Don’t study AI. Use it on one real task you already do.

Pick a chore you know well, like a weekly email, a messy spreadsheet or a meal plan, and try it there. Because you know what good looks like, you’ll see right away when it helps and when it doesn’t. No course can give you that.

Ten minutes on a real task teaches more than an hour of videos.

Try this

Find your one task

Here’s what a normal week looks like for me: [describe your work or your chores]. Suggest three small ways you could help, and one thing I shouldn’t use you for.

You’ll see a few ideas, and at least one you could try today.

Why: a task you know well is the fastest teacher, because you can judge the answer.

The catch

Early doesn’t mean slow. Some workplaces already expect people to use these tools, and that can feel like pressure.

Go at your own pace. Just don’t wait for things to settle down. They won’t for a while.

Remember

Don’t study AI. Use it on one real task you already do, and learn from that.
